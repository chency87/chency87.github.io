I am a Ph.D. student in [Computing Science at Simon Fraser University](https://www.sfu.ca/fas/computing.html), co-advised by Prof. [Jiannan Wang](https://dbgroup.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/jnwang/) and Prof. [Zhengjie Miao](https://www.miaozhengjie.com/). My research focuses on data management, particularly SQL query equivalence and reliable Text-to-SQL. I develop methods to verify the correctness of SQL queries generated by large language models and explore how combining lightweight models can improve Text-to-SQL accuracy.


<!--more-->

## Research interests

- **SQL query equivalence** — Developing methods to prove or refute equivalence between SQL queries, with applications to query rewriting and validation of generated SQL.
- **Text-to-SQL evaluation** — Using targeted test databases and counterexamples to assess the correctness of generated SQL and diagnose errors in LLM reasoning.
- **LLM ensembles** — Exploring how to combine outputs from smaller language models to improve the accuracy of the overall system.
